Anchor a Geomacro risk-state proof on Arc mainnet.

This small experiment reads Geomacro's current machine-readable intelligence state, hashes the proof payload locally, then lets the connected wallet anchor that hash in a zero-value Arc mainnet transaction. The transaction spends only Arc gas in real USDC.

Safety boundary: this page does not enable Geomacro's production x402/payment rails, does not hold a private key, and does not authorize a business action. The wallet signs and broadcasts directly.

What is written onchain?

The transaction calldata contains a compact UTF-8 record:

GEOMACRO_ARC_MAINNET_V1|<country>|<state_version>|<sha256_proof>

The transaction is sent to the connected wallet itself with value 0. The proof is public and permanent. Do not put secrets or personal data in the payload.
